TV Compatibility
The Avantree HT5009 supports TVs with optical (TOSLINK), AUX (3.5mm), RCA input cables, or built-in Bluetooth audio outputs. Before purchasing, confirm that your TV has one of these options. With the HT5009, you'll be able to listen to your favorite TV shows & movies at your own volume without disturbing those around you.
Using with Smart TVs
For Smart TVs with built-in Bluetooth (e.g., 2024 Samsung TVs), the HT5009 headphones can pair directly via Bluetooth. In this case, slide the transmitter to 'OFF' because there is no need to use the transmitter.
Hassle-Free Set Up
There’s no need to go through the complex Bluetooth pairing process; simply plug the HT5009’s transmitter dock into your TV’s audio output port, power on both the dock and the headphone, they’ll quickly connect with each other, and you should be good to go. Works brilliantly with older Non-Smart TVs as well.
Perfectly Synced Visual-Audio
With "FastStream" Bluetooth built-in, the HT5009 provides
<
40 ms audio latency – significantly lower and better than the 200+ ms of typical Bluetooth solutions on the market. With this feature, no matter what content you're watching, the audio and visual will always be perfectly synchronized.
Soundbar/Stereo AVR Support
The “Pass-through” feature of Avantree HT5009 allows it to work TOGETHER with your existing Soundbar or Stereo AV Receivers WHILE sharing the same audio output port on TV; hear the TV at your own volume through the headphones while your family/friends listen through the speakers.
Long Range, Stay Connected
Equipped with Bluetooth v5.0 and the highest Bluetooth range, Class 1, HT5009 ensures stable & reliable connectivity of up to 164 ft (50 m, barring any excessive physical/wireless interference). Need to start making dinner? No problem, do what you need to do without worrying about the audio cutting out.
Easy to use - no pairing required
A truly plug & play experience - simply turn on the transmitter and 'headphones and they will automatically reconnect. No need to worry about complicated pairing procedures.
No audio lag synchronized experience
Ideal for watching TV, movies and gaming, our headphones deliver high quality sound in perfect synchronization. Our transmitter also features dual link technology allowing you to add a second pair of headphones and share your listening experience. (Note: To ensure there's no lip sync delay, your 2nd headphone must also support the FastStream codec. Consider the Avantree NB16, AH6, AS90, AS9 which support FastStream).
Hear audio through headphones and external speaker
If your TV only has one optical output port, it can be a pain to connect any new devices. With the Avantree Oasis Plus, you can connect it between your soundbar and your TV to play audio from the soundbar and stream to your headphones simultaneously!
With the simple flip of a switch, you can choose between Bluetooth mode and pass-through (wired mode) without having to plug or unplug any wires.
Extended range
Featuring Bluetooth Class I technology, our Oasis Plus transmitter's signal reaches up to 164 feet / 50m! Please note the range is dependent upon physical obstructions and wireless interference.
Extended playtime
After charging for 3.5 hours, your headphones will allow you to indulge in your movies or music for up to 40 hours! The transmitter can be powered using any USB port on your TV and powers on and off automatically when the TV is turned on or off.

15 Q&A complètes pour la page FAQ dédiée (distincte de la FAQ courte home et de la FAQ 10 Q&A produit — recoupements assumés, réponses identiques pour rester cohérent).

1. Does it need electricity? No. It's insulated, not heated. The roof and door flap are lined with a reflective film that reflects your cat's own body heat back into the shelter, and the walls are padded for extra insulation. There's nothing to plug in or charge.

2. Is it waterproof? The roof and door flap are water-resistant and built to shed rain, and the raised legs keep the base off wet ground. We're calling it water-resistant rather than waterproof until we've tested a sample ourselves in real UK weather.

3. What size should I choose? Size L (55 × 45 × 40 cm) is our main size and the one currently in stock. Size M (50 × 40 × 36 cm) is smaller and only available when our supplier can confirm stock — check the product page for current availability.

4. Will it protect my cat from rain and damp? Yes, that's a core part of the design: a water-resistant roof and door flap, plus raised legs that lift the shelter off wet or muddy ground, where cardboard and unlined shelters usually fail first.

5. Will it protect my cat from foxes? No shelter can guarantee that, and we won't tell you it does. What it gives your cat is an enclosed, private space to retreat into. For context, Cats Protection's own figures show fox attacks on cats are uncommon — around 5 in every 10,000 vet visits, compared with 541 for fights with other cats.

6. Does it need power for anything? No. It's a passive, insulated shelter. There's no heating element, no battery and nothing electrical inside it.

7. How do I clean it? We're finalising exact care instructions after testing a sample — this answer will be updated once confirmed. In general, a wipe-down of the outer fabric is expected to be enough for everyday use.

8. Is it suitable for summer as well as winter? Yes. The insulation works both ways — it moderates temperature rather than only adding heat — and the raised legs and ventilated design help keep it from becoming a stuffy box in warm weather. If you don't need it in summer, it folds flat for storage.

9. Can more than one cat use it? It's designed and sized for one cat to use comfortably. We'll update capacity guidance once we've tested a sample with real dimensions confirmed.

10. How long does assembly take, and what tools do I need? No tools. It opens into shape in a couple of minutes and folds flat again just as quickly.

11. How long does delivery take? 6–10 business days. It ships from our supplier's warehouse, and the delivery estimate reflects that — it isn't a same-day dispatch product.

12. Is delivery free? Yes, free UK delivery on every order.

13. What's your returns policy? You have 30 days to return it if it isn't right for you or your cat. See our Returns & refunds policy for the full process, including your legal 14-day cancellation right under UK consumer law.

14. How do refunds work? Once we've received your return, we'll process your refund to your original payment method. Full details, including timing, are in our Returns & refunds policy.

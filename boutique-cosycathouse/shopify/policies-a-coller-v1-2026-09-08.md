## Returns & refunds

```html
<p><strong>Your legal right to cancel (14 days)</strong> If you're a consumer shopping from the UK, you have a legal right under the Consumer Contracts Regulations to cancel your order within <strong>14 days</strong> of receiving it, without giving a reason. To cancel, contact us at info@ohventures.fr within this period. You'll then need to return the item within 14 days of telling us you want to cancel.</p>
<p><strong>Our returns policy (30 days)</strong> On top of your legal cancellation right, we offer <strong>30 days</strong> from the date of delivery to return your item if it isn't right for you or your cat — for example, if it doesn't fit where you expected, or your cat won't settle into it even after trying our adoption guide.</p>
<p><strong>Condition of returned items</strong> Items should be returned unused and in a condition suitable for resale, in their original packaging where possible. This doesn't affect your legal rights if the product is faulty.</p>
<p><strong>How to return an item</strong> Email info@ohventures.fr with your order number to start a return. We'll confirm the return address and next steps.</p>
<p><strong>Refunds</strong> Once we've received and checked your returned item, we'll process your refund to your original payment method. Refunds are typically processed within 14 days of us receiving the return, in line with UK consumer law.</p>
<p><strong>Faulty or damaged items</strong> If your item arrives faulty or damaged, contact us at info@ohventures.fr with photos and your order number as soon as possible. Under the <strong>Consumer Rights Act 2015</strong>, you're entitled to a repair, replacement or refund for faulty goods, and this sits alongside — not instead of — the return options above.</p>
<p><strong>Return shipping costs</strong> Return postage is paid by the customer, unless the item is faulty, damaged or not as described — in that case we cover it.</p>
```

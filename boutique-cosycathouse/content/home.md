## 2. Pain — "When the first frost comes, where does your cat sleep?"

**Section title:** When the first frost comes, where does your cat sleep?

**Situation 1 — your cat sleeps outside more than you'd like**
Your cat comes and goes as it pleases. Lately it's staying out later, even as the evenings turn cold. You'd rather it came in — but it doesn't always want to.

**Situation 2 — you feed a cat that isn't fully yours**
You put food out for a cat that visits, or a small colony near you. Feeding is easy. Giving it somewhere dry and warm to sleep is harder.

**Situation 3 — your cat is getting older**
Older cats feel the cold more, and a stiff, damp night outside doesn't help. You want somewhere raised and insulated, not a cardboard box that gets wet through.

## 5. "Will my cat actually use it?"

**Section title:** Will my cat actually use it?

**Intro line:** Most cats don't walk straight into a new shelter. A few small steps make a big difference.

**3 steps:**
1. **Place it somewhere your cat already goes** — near a spot it naps in, not a new corner of the garden.
2. **Add something that smells familiar** — a worn blanket or your cat's own bedding, not new fabric.
3. **Leave food near the entrance for the first few days** — it turns "strange new object" into "somewhere good happens."

## 8. FAQ (short — 5 Q&A)

**Does it need electricity?**
No. The roof and door flap are lined with a reflective film that reflects your cat's own body heat back at them. There's nothing to plug in.

**Will it keep the rain out?**
The roof and door flap are water-resistant, and the raised legs keep the base off wet ground. It's built for typical UK weather, not for standing water.

**Will it protect my cat from foxes?**
No shelter can guarantee that. Cats Protection's own data shows fox attacks on cats are rare — about 5 in every 10,000 vet visits. This shelter gives your cat a private, enclosed space to retreat to; it isn't a fox-proof structure.

**What if my cat doesn't use it at first?**
That's normal. Our free guide walks you through placement, scent and food to help your cat adopt it — most cats take a few days to a couple of weeks.

**How long does delivery take?**
6–10 business days, shipped from our supplier's warehouse. Delivery is free across the UK.

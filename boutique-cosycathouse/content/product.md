## Long description

### Warmth without electricity

The roof and door flap are lined with a reflective film. It works passively: it reflects your cat's own body heat back into the shelter instead of letting it escape into the night air. There's nothing to plug in, nothing to charge, and nothing that can run out of power on the coldest night. The walls are padded too, adding a second layer of insulation around your cat.

### Dry and off the ground

Cold isn't the only problem outdoors — damp is just as hard on a cat. This shelter stands on raised plastic legs, lifting it above wet grass, mud and standing water. The roof and door flap are water-resistant, built to shed rain rather than let it soak through. It's designed for the kind of weather the UK actually gets, not for a display shelf.

### Your cat adopts it

A new shelter can sit untouched for the first few days — that's normal, not a fault. We've put together a short guide, *Help your cat adopt its shelter*, covering where to place it, what to add so it smells familiar, and how to use food to encourage that first visit. It's included free with every order. [Link to guide page]

### Safety and foxes

No outdoor shelter can promise total protection from every animal, and we won't claim this one does. What it gives your cat is an enclosed, private space to retreat into, off the ground and out of the open. For context: Cats Protection's own figures show fox attacks on cats are rare — around 5 in every 10,000 vet visits, compared with 541 for fights with other cats. We'd rather give you the real numbers than an empty promise.

### Assembly and folding

No tools, no instructions to lose. The shelter opens into shape in a couple of minutes and folds flat again when you need to store it, move it, or clean underneath it.

## FAQ (10 Q&A)

**1. Does it need electricity to keep my cat warm?**
No. It's insulated, not heated. The reflective lining and padded walls work passively, with nothing to plug in.

**2. Is it waterproof?**
The roof and door flap are water-resistant and built to shed rain, and the raised legs keep the base off wet ground. We're not calling it fully waterproof until we've tested a sample ourselves in real UK rain.

**3. Will it protect my cat from foxes?**
It gives your cat an enclosed, private space to retreat to — not a guarantee against any animal. Cats Protection's data shows fox attacks on cats are uncommon (about 5 in 10,000 vet visits). We won't claim "fox-proof."

**4. How many cats can it fit?**
It's designed for one cat to use comfortably. We'll confirm exact capacity once we've tested a sample.

**5. How long does assembly take, and do I need tools?**
No tools needed. It opens into shape in a couple of minutes.
